No. French Equity Partners is not a licensed real estate broker or sales associate in the State of Florida, and we do not act as an agent for you or represent your interests in a fiduciary capacity. We purchase real estate directly, as a principal buyer, under the private-purchaser exemption in Florida Statute §475.011 — the same basis on which any individual or company can buy real estate for their own account. Because we are not licensed, we do not list, broker, or market real estate on behalf of a seller or any third party; any offer we present is to purchase the real estate ourselves or to assign our rights under a purchase contract. We recommend every seller consult an independent Florida real estate attorney or licensed agent before signing any agreement, and we're glad to close through a licensed title company of your choosing.

When we don't close on a property ourselves, what we market to other buyers is our contractual right to purchase it — our equitable interest under the purchase agreement — not the real estate itself. We never represent that we own, list, or have authority to sell property on a seller's behalf. This distinction is required under Florida law for anyone operating without a real estate license.

Our offers reflect the real estate's current condition, local market data, and the certainty and speed of a direct cash sale.
