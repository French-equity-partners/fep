FR/BAR "AS IS" contract: the assignability option

The FR/BAR contract is a Florida Realtors form supplied by the listing agent, so it isn't stored here. When you make an offer:

  1. Buyer name: French Equity Partners LLC and/or assigns.
  2. In Section 7 (Assignability), tick "may assign and thereby be released from any further liability" (or "may assign but not be released", if the seller insists). Never leave "may not assign" ticked if you plan to assign.
  3. Tell the agent in writing: "Buyer is an investor and may assign this contract or close simultaneously."
  4. Upload the signed contract as a photo/PDF note on the deal, and tick "Contract signed" in the checklist.
If the seller won't allow assignment, use a double close instead (agreed with Nicholas). Each deal with Nicholas gets its own property-specific JV agreement; save it on the deal.
