FEP CRM - upload instructions
1. In GitHub, open French-equity-partners/fep -> Add file -> Upload files.
2. Upload crm.html and deal-entry.html (replace the old deal-entry.html).
3. Commit. Open https://frenchequitypartners.com/crm.html and sign in with your usual account
   (patientacquisitionhub@gmail.com or julien@camdenacquisitionhub.com).
4. Import tab: download the DBPR region files (Region 1-10 + Orange, NOT Hillsborough/Region 13)
   and upload them there. Re-run monthly to refresh licences; notes and stages are kept.
