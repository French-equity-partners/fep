Tax deed lien research: learning plan and report template

An 8-week plan to learn pre-auction lien research, plus the report template to practise on. Example county: Hillsborough, Florida. Swap in your own.

8-week plan

6–8 hours a week. Tick weeks off as you go.

WeeksFocusDone
1–2Read Florida Statutes chapter 197 and your county's tax deed rules; learn every record website below
3–4Title search course basics; full analyses of 5 past auction properties
5–610 more past-auction analyses using the template; 1-hour attorney check of your lien logic
7–8Fix gaps; offer first free reports to real bidders for feedback and testimonials
